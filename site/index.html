02 What makes it different

Not a drawing minigame.

SuperInk is built around one rule: drawing must remain connected to positioning, resources, weapon behavior, and player expression.

01Tactical creation

Drawing stays inside combat.

The player can keep moving while the scroll is open, but gives up normal shooting and attention. Where and when you draw matters as much as accuracy.

02Player-authored language

You practice your own pattern.

Pattern slots are designed to let players replace a default template, then learn to reproduce their own visual shorthand under pressure.

03Fair mastery

Quality changes reliability—not damage.

A stronger drawing improves formation, ammo, durability, or stability. Mechanical skill is rewarded without turning accuracy into runaway DPS.

04Playable failure

A bad drawing creates a new problem.

An imperfect input may still form an unstable gun that jams or expires early. Failure changes the encounter instead of ending at a red error message.

One closed tactical economy

03 Recognition algorithm

Explainable by design.

SuperInk uses a tunable geometric pipeline rather than black-box image classification. Every step has a visible design purpose and a testable output.

Why this step exists

Preserve the player’s motion.

The drawing canvas records ordered stroke points instead of flattening the input into a screenshot. Stroke boundaries, path order, and motion remain available to the recognizer.
